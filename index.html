Questions

How long does my spot stay up?

For the life of the wall. Approved spots are never resold or replaced, and they only come down if they break the Community Rules. If the wall is ever retired, every owner gets a full-size picture of the final wall and their Certificate of Ownership, so nobody loses what they bought.

When am I charged?

Your card is checked when you order and charged only after your spot is approved. If it isn't approved, you're never charged.

What can I put on my spot?

A drawing, picture, logo, or color, plus a short caption. Keep it friendly for all ages. No links, phone numbers, or social handles.

Can I buy more than one?

Yes. Add as many spots as you like to your cart and pay once.

What's a Founding Pixel?

The first 1,000 spots claimed get a gold Founding badge on the wall, in Explore, and on their certificate. When they're gone, they're gone.

Who can buy?

Anyone 13 or older. If you're under 18, you need a parent or guardian's OK.

Community Rules

These rules apply to everyone who uses Pixel Purchase, including buyers, commenters, and businesses.

  1. Eligibility. You must be at least 13 years old to use Pixel Purchase. If you are under 18, you must have permission from a parent or legal guardian before placing an order.
  2. Content standards. All pixels, captions, comments, and profiles must be suitable for a general audience. Content that is hateful, harassing, sexual, violent, or threatening is not permitted.
  3. Contact information and links. Links, web addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, social media handles, and payment app details are not permitted anywhere on Pixel Purchase.
  4. Solicitation. Giveaways, requests for money, and requests to make contact outside Pixel Purchase are not permitted.
  5. Ownership. You may only post images, drawings, logos, and text that you created, own, or have permission to use. Businesses may post their own logos and branding. Photographs of other people require their consent.
  6. Review and payment. Every order is reviewed before it appears on the wall. Your payment method is authorized when you place an order and is charged only if your order is approved. If it is not approved, you are not charged.
  7. How long spots last. Approved pixels stay on the wall for as long as Pixel Purchase operates, and a claimed spot is never resold or replaced. Pixel Purchase may remove any pixel, comment, or profile that violates these rules, without refund, and may suspend the account responsible. If the wall is ever retired, every owner will receive a full-size image of the final wall and their Certificate of Ownership.
  8. Likes and awards. Pixel of the Day is awarded each day to the pixel with the most likes that day, and a pixel may win more than once. Likes must come from genuine accounts. Buying, trading, or otherwise inflating likes results in disqualification.
  9. Find the Pixel. The fastest time recorded each day by a signed-in player is posted at midnight Central Time.
  10. Changes. These rules may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Pixel Purchase means you accept the current rules.
